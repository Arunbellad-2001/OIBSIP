# OIBSIP Data Science Task 2 - Unemployment Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is part of my Data Science internship at Oasis Infobyte.

In this task, I analyzed the unemployment rate in different regions of India
during 2020.

The main aim of this project is to understand unemployment, employment and
labour participation rates using data analysis and visualization.

## Dataset

The dataset used in this project is the **Unemployment in India** dataset.

The dataset contains information about different regions of India from
January 2020 to October 2020.

Main columns used in the analysis:

- Region
- Date
- Estimated Unemployment Rate (%)
- Estimated Employed
- Estimated Labour Participation Rate (%)

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Data Analysis

The following steps were performed in this project:

1. Loaded the unemployment dataset.
2. Checked the shape and information of the dataset.
3. Checked for missing values.
4. Cleaned the column names.
5. Converted the Date column into datetime format.
6. Calculated descriptive statistics.
7. Analyzed unemployment rates by region.
8. Found the top 10 regions with the highest average unemployment.
9. Analyzed monthly unemployment rates.
10. Analyzed monthly employment.
11. Analyzed monthly labour participation rates.
12. Created a heatmap of unemployment rates by region and month.
13. Found the top 10 regions with the lowest average unemployment.
14. Compared unemployment rate with labour participation rate.
15. Performed correlation analysis.

## Visualizations

The project includes the following visualizations:

- Top 10 regions with highest average unemployment rate
- Monthly unemployment rate trend
- Monthly employment trend
- Monthly labour participation rate trend
- Unemployment rate by region and month heatmap
- Top 10 regions with lowest average unemployment rate
- Unemployment rate vs labour participation rate scatter plot
- Correlation heatmap

## Key Findings

- Haryana had the highest average unemployment rate at about 27.48%.
- Meghalaya had the lowest average unemployment rate at about 3.87%.
- Unemployment increased sharply during April and May 2020.
- The highest monthly average unemployment rate was about 23.24% in May.
- Employment reached its lowest monthly average in April at about 10.57 million.
- Labour participation was lowest in April at about 35.30%.
- Unemployment started to decrease after May in most regions.
- Unemployment rates were different across different regions.

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how unemployment changed across different
regions of India during 2020.

The analysis showed a sharp increase in unemployment during April and May.
Employment and labour participation also decreased during this period.

After May, the unemployment rate started to decrease and employment started
to recover.
